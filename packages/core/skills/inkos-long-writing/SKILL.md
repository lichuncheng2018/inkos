# Long-form narrative craft

One method, divided by the job in hand: building the foundation, planning a chapter, writing it, settling its state, reviewing it, revising it. Apply the part that belongs to the current operation.

- A chapter is made of scenes. A scene has an immediate objective, resistance, a meaningful turn, and consequences that survive it.
- Character choices follow what each person wants, knows, fears, and can afford. Relationships change through events, not author explanation.
- Preserve the user's voice, viewpoint, prohibitions, proportions, and current direction. Genre convention is only a default when the governed context is silent.
- Use references as evidence for craft or facts only. Do not reproduce source wording, names, scene order, or signature combinations.
- When the author changes a premise, distinguish retained source facts from superseded generated plans. Apply the latest explicit change consistently to the foundation, chapter plan and prose; an old memo or hook cannot require restoring a deleted premise. Report remaining contradictions between those documents instead of treating the obsolete plan as a new instruction.

<!-- audience: architect -->
## Foundation

- When creating a foundation, connect a visible foreground line to a deeper causal line, give every volume an observable end-state and irreversible change, make character arcs pay a cost, and distinguish dormant seeds from already active promises.
- When reviewing a new foundation, test whether the central conflict can sustain the requested span, the opening creates forward pressure, the world is specific and coherent, major characters differ in motive and voice, and the outline can progress without repeating one beat. For fan fiction or series work, preserve source facts while establishing a real divergence or new narrative space.

## Chapter planning

- A chapter memo starts from the chapter goal: one sentence naming what has changed for the reader by the end of the chapter (progress, a gain, a breakthrough, a shift in a relationship or position), not a summary of what happens. Anchor it in what readers of this genre came for (in cultivation fiction: practice, techniques and spells, pills and spirit stones, breakthroughs, fights, opportunities). When the volume direction for this chapter centres on chores or errands, find the progress inside them and make that the goal.
- The writer gives every line of the memo about the same room, so the shape of the memo becomes the shape of the chapter. A dated list of small events comes out as a summary of days. Plan a few scenes instead: one or two are played out as short beats of exchange between people, and carry most of the chapter; everything else is passed over in a line that says how much time went by and what it changed.
- Every scene advances the goal, obstructs it, or shows its result or cost. Leave out events that do none of these, including side errands added only for texture.
- Side characters arrive with their own wants, and the business the memo gives them touches the goal: they compete for the protagonist's time, measure themselves against him, obstruct him, trade with him, or notice the change. The world's rules, prices, quotas and deadlines reach the reader through what these people say, with exact amounts and units. Transactions list unit price, quantity and total, not "quite a few".
- Show progress through both visible results and the decisive process that produces them. When a cultivation bottleneck or breakthrough, first use of a technique, decisive combat, or consequential choice turns on an inner change, plan that change as playable beats: the immediate aim, resistance, relevant bodily, qi, sensory or understanding shift, the choice or insight, and its cost or result. Thought, sensation, resolve and emotion belong in beats when they shape a choice, reveal pressure or change the reader's experience. Routine repetition, travel, waiting and taking stock may be passed over with duration and outcome.
- Give the chapter an earned emotional movement. After a meaningful gain, danger, revelation or loss, plan the character's immediate reaction and any resulting change in confidence, trust, relationship or resolve. The shift may be small and must fit the work's tone; do not add conflict or sentiment for its own sake.
- A cheat or system notice and the choice it offers take one or two beats; do not spend beats reciting the full rule sheet or projecting later doublings.
- List what happens. A constraint the writer must not break, and the figures that have to reconcile, go into a short notes block the writer uses only for checking.
- Tie each required piece of information to the event that reveals it, and list only the rules this chapter actually uses. Where canon is silent on a small concrete fact such as an amount, a price, an object or a neighbour, pick one and keep it consistent; do not forbid specifics.
- Do not use function labels such as "transmigrate", "activate the cheat", "keep a low profile", or "test the system" as events; fold identity and rules into the task already underway. Do not draft finished prose lines for the writer to copy, and do not set wording quotas such as how often a name, catchphrase or connective appears.
- The last scene stops once the matter is settled or a fresh pressure has just surfaced, not on a mechanical cliffhanger. Show the after-effect of a major payoff before starting another escalation cycle.

## Prose

- Write the memo's scenes as things that happen in front of the reader: who is there, what each of them wants, what is said and done, and what comes of it. Reveal setting and backstory through action, evidence, dialogue, and sensory particulars. A synopsis or an analysis does not stand in for a scene.
- Every passage alters conflict, evidence, emotion, relationship, knowledge, position, or future consequence. Remove padding rather than stretching to a number.
- Control information deliberately: answer some active reader questions, deepen others through concrete evidence, and do not manufacture twists by hiding facts the viewpoint should know.
- In an exchange, each reply that matters changes leverage, reveals a constraint, creates a debt, or forces a choice. Subtext comes from what a character risks by speaking, withholding, or changing the subject.
- Prefer concrete verbs, observable reactions, distinct voices, and readable paragraph rhythm over narrator conclusions, repeated hedges, decorative description, or synchronized crowd reactions.
- End a chapter after a material change or fresh pressure, not with a mechanical cliffhanger formula. Show the after-effect of major payoffs before starting another escalation cycle.
- Sentences in the memo, the references and the Skills supply information; the prose says it in its own words.

### 中文正文

中文正文默认这样写。用户指定的文风、作品的文风约定和已激活的文风 Skill 说到的地方，以它们为准。

- 用词朴素、口语自然，但朴素不等于平淡：文笔来自准确的细节、人物自己的眼光和有起伏的节奏，不靠修饰语、比喻和抒情。用常用词把人、事和因果说清楚，主语和动作明确；普通动作用普通动词。
- 戏写在当场。展开的一场可以是人与人交锋，也可以是人物面对功法、环境或自身局限；写清当下目标、具体阻力、行动与反馈，最后如何改变局面或人物。人物交锋要写对方如何回应，独自行动要让尝试、感知/理解变化、选择和后果构成过程。规矩、价钱、名额和期限，多从人物嘴里带着数目说出来；叙述者也可以直接交代必要的背景、规则、价格和人物动机，讲到读者明白就回到眼前的事。
- 日常重复的修炼、赶路、干活可以写明用了多久、到了哪一步、换来什么，再用一两句带过。遇到瓶颈、突破、悟法、首次施展、关键斗法或必须承担代价的选择时，若体内变化、感知或领悟决定结果，就把关键过程写在当场；写到改变选择或局势的那一步即可，不逐轮复现重复动作。隔了几天，用一句话跨过去，带上这段时间里变了的那样东西。数目和账在发生的那一刻说一次：人物此刻手里有多少、还差多少。
- 细节要专属于此时此地此人。写人和环境时，挑一两样能看出处境的具体东西：物件的磨损和价钱、声音、气味、人物手上正在做的事。一处写准胜过三处泛写，也不必给每个动作配一组感官描写。
- 让人物的出身、职业和性格决定他先注意什么、怎么下判断：精明的人先看成色和价钱，谨慎的人先看退路。前世经历只在贴切处化成一句判断或比方，一个场景一两处即可。
- 情感要有因由，主要通过处境和动作带出来：人物做了什么、停在哪里、注意到什么、说了什么，需要时再直接点明一句感受。得了好处时的喜悦、期待和轻松照常写；警惕和克制要有当下的具体理由。
- 心理活动用人物当下会想的话，带他自己的口气：嘀咕、自嘲、算小账、简短自问，围绕眼前的得失、疑问、愿望或决定。现代经验和对自己“开挂”的自知，落到人物自己的具体经历上。
- 对话按人物此时想问、想说、想隐瞒的事组织，措辞自然，反应明确。普通的称呼和说话提示可以重复；人物的差别来自态度、目的和各自知道的事。独处的场景不为凑对话加人。
- 按一个意思或一次反应分段，以短段为主，长短句交替：要紧的瞬间用短句或单句段停一下，铺陈处一段可以写满几句。相邻几段换着起笔：物件、声音、对话、结果、时间。单句成段留给真正的转折或停顿。
- 人物照常做事时顺带看出变化，比如搬得动东西了、听得清隔壁的动静了。用户或章纲没有要求时，不为解释设定专门编排实验、核验或仪式。
- 穿越、重生类开篇从一个具体的处境或动作切入，身份和记忆在后面几段里顺带交代。

## Review

Read the chapter the way its reader will before measuring it against the plan. Report what that reader would trip over, with the lines as evidence. An empty list is a valid result.

- Continuity: facts, time, places, resources, who knows what, and the state of open promises agree with the governed context and earlier chapters. Figures reconcile.
- The goal: by the end of the chapter the change its memo names has happened where the reader can see it. Name any stretch that serves no part of that goal.
- Scenes: the turning points are played out as scenes. Report passages that summarise day after day, restate an account already given, or extend sensation and reflection without changing a choice, revealing pressure or making a consequential process legible.
- Movement: the reader's feeling rises and falls with the events. Report a payoff or a blow told in the same even tone as routine business, a gain that brings no pleasure or a danger that brings no fear, a turning point nobody present reacts to, and a chapter whose scenes all run at one temperature.
- Characters act from what they want, know and can afford. Nobody turns careless or competent because the plot needs it.
- Voice: one narrating voice through the chapter, the one the Work has chosen — the user's instruction, then the author's stated intent, then the style guide, then the active voice Skill. What that voice allows is not a defect.
- Diagnose the smallest layer that actually causes a problem. A formatting failure is not evidence that the prose is bad.

中文正文里常见的机器痕迹，见到就按原文行报出：

- 情绪套话：心跳加速、深吸一口气、倒吸一口凉气、嘴角露出一丝笑意、眼前一亮、猛地睁开眼、愣住了、强迫自己冷静；为一种情绪配一整套呼吸、指节、喉头和肌肉的细描；一处反应接一处身上的小感觉（耳根发热、心里一热、指尖一僵、背后冒汗、胸口一松）来报情绪；替人物写一段想来想去、自己宽慰自己的心理，而那里本可以让他对人说一句话；只有谨慎而没有合理的情感。
- 通用描写：放进任何一本书都成立的“破旧的屋顶”“粗糙的衣服”“头痛欲裂”；精雕的比喻；矛盾意象、营业口吻和管理术语。
- 替读者下结论：描写或算账之后补一句“就这些了”“这账算得明明白白”“这就是他的全部家当”；一段叙述后单独起一行的一句话结论；句末金句。
- 机器句式：“不是……而是……”式对照，“手还是这双手，屋子还是这间屋子，可……”式重复，“没有……，没有……，……便……”式排比收束。
- 念账和念设定：把一整套规则一句一段念出来；同一笔账换一种清单重复登记；把账本、规划、验收用语照搬成正文；像盘点或测试报告的段落。
- 节奏：连续几段用同一主语加动作起笔；把长句拆行冒充节奏；“正想着，天色渐渐暗了下来”式的空过场；“醒来—不对—记忆涌入—穿越了”的固定开篇流程。
- 人物：人人说俏皮话或警句；替人物写复盘报告、抽象的人生判断；“穿越必有金手指”之类人人都能写的话；每个念头都套职业比喻。

## Revision

- Diagnose and repair the smallest layer that actually causes the problem. A formatting failure is not evidence that the prose is bad.
- During prose polishing, change only wording, sentence shape, paragraphing, sensory precision, punctuation, and dialogue naturalness. Preserve events, viewpoint, character decisions, information, and consequences. Keep the result close in length unless instructed otherwise. Preserve a voice explicitly chosen by the user; when changing style, apply the requested voice throughout the authorized scope. Existing generated wording is not itself a voice constraint.
- A passage flagged as machine-written usually fails for one particular reason; find it before rewriting. The usual ones: days or routine summarised in tidy sequence, each step joined to the next (first, then, once that was done, only then); an account restated after it was already given; a rule or a procedure recited in full, by the narrator or by a character who talks like a posted notice; bodily sensation or reflection drawn out in slow motion; progress stated without a figure, a duration or a witness; a paragraph longer than what it carries; a conclusion drawn for the reader. Repair that cause where it sits: open on one moment and fold the routine into a clause; bring in the person who was there, let him say only the part that touches his own business, the way he would say it, and let the other cut in; keep the step that decides the matter and drop the rest; give the elapsed time and the result; state the figure once; split the paragraph; or cut the line. Rewording a passage whose cause is left in place does not change how it reads.
- A replacement has to sit in the chapter as if it had always been there: the same narrator, paragraph length and way of talking as the paragraphs around it that nobody flagged.
